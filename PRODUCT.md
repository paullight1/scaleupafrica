# Product

## Register

product

(Split-register project: the landing page (`/`) is a brand surface and should be treated with brand-register standards when worked on directly; the app surfaces — directory, profile creation, funding — are the product register and the default.)

## Users

African SME founders and young entrepreneurs. Context: mostly on mid/low-end Android phones over variable networks; time-poor; often discovering the product through social links. Job to be done: (1) get their business visible in a credible pan-African directory, and (2) find real, current funding opportunities (grants, fellowships, accelerators) they qualify for without trawling dozens of funder sites.

## Product Purpose

Cresciva is a public SME directory plus a subscription-gated, AI-curated funding-intelligence feed. Success looks like: founders complete a profile, return to check funding opportunities, and trust the results enough to pay for access.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy & empowering. Credible, institutional-grade, "we open doors" — the product handles founders' business identity and paid funding advice, so confidence and legitimacy outrank flash. Voice: direct, encouraging, never hypey.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated SaaS landing pages (gradient text, identical card grids, uppercase eyebrow labels on every section).
- Scammy "get funding fast" lead-gen sites — nothing that undermines trust in the funding data.
- Heavy, animation-laden pages that crawl on low-end devices.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust is the product** — provenance, disclaimers, and honest states (sample vs live data) must always be explicit; never blur the line between AI-curated and verified information.
2. **Mobile network reality** — every screen must be usable on a mid-range Android over 3G; performance is a design feature.
3. **The profile is the founder's storefront** — profile creation and directory cards deserve the most craft; they represent real businesses.
4. **Earn the subscription** — free surfaces should demonstrate concrete value, not tease with lorem-quality filler.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target: ≥4.5:1 body contrast, full keyboard operability, screen-reader labels on interactive elements. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Design for small viewports first.
