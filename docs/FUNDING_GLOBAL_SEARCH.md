# Free global grant research

This is the research guide for `find-and-verify-global-grants`. Use it with
FUNDING_DISCOVERY.md and the latest dated verification evidence. Discovery is
broader than the RSS feeds; it uses existing connected search tools and free public
pages. Never purchase access, invoke paid search/extraction or the Lovable engine,
or add a provider subscription. Stop and report unavailable access or entitlement.

## Coverage per run

Start with new RSS candidates and programmes not already represented by their
official source/current cycle. Aim to screen up to 20 distinct candidates and fully
verify up to 12, within existing free access and usage allowances. These are research
targets, not publication quotas. An unsuccessful search is a valid recorded result.

Use at least six focused searches spanning two African regions and three sectors.
Rotate groups based on the last evidence file rather than repeating the same first
results every day. Include English, French and Portuguese discovery terms across
the searches; translate only to understand, retaining original deadline wording.
Include current year and the coming year where appropriate, then inspect current
official pages rather than trusting dates in indexed search snippets.

| Region | Example country terms to rotate |
| --- | --- |
| West | Nigeria, Ghana, Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, Benin, Cabo Verde |
| East | Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Ethiopia, Mauritius |
| Central | Cameroon, Democratic Republic of Congo, Chad, Gabon |
| Southern | South Africa, Zambia, Malawi, Mozambique, Namibia, Angola |
| North | Morocco, Egypt, Tunisia, Algeria, Mauritania |
| Global | worldwide, international, developing countries, Africa eligible |

Rotate climate/biodiversity; agriculture/food; health/research; women/human rights;
arts/culture; civic participation; education/institutions; and enterprise/digital
innovation. Search individual, informal-team, nonprofit and SME programmes rather
than assuming every opportunity is a business grant.

Useful discovery query patterns:

- `grant call applications [sector] [country/region] [year] official`
- `research grant worldwide applications [year] deadline`
- `appel à projets subvention [secteur] Afrique [année] candidature`
- `appel à candidatures financement organisations [pays] date limite`
- `subvenções candidaturas [setor] África [ano] prazo`
- `financiamento não reembolsável concurso [país] candidaturas`

Use aggregators as leads only. Follow official programme pages and the portals
they actually link. Official watchlist: IKI, IDRC, CODESRIA, Enabel, NRF South
Africa, African Culture Fund, Interledger, Microsoft Research, IHS, WomenStrong,
Rufford, Nature Forward, Awesome Foundation's worldwide thematic chapters,
Pollock-Krasner Foundation, Front Line Defenders and Urgent Action Fund-Africa.
The watchlist is not a claim of current intake or universal African eligibility.

## Verification and preservation

Read the complete programme-specific eligibility, current invitation and official
application route. A future deadline, working generic login or absent deadline
alone is insufficient to establish open intake. A normal authenticated application
gateway is acceptable when the official current call explicitly invites applications
and the linked gateway loads; record that authentication limits inspection.

Keep exact published deadline text. Store a UTC instant/timezone only when the
funder supplies enough information. Distinguish submission deadlines, questions,
eligibility screening, invited full proposals and later review cycles. Recurring
programmes may have chapter-specific deadlines: Awesome Conservation and Climate
has published review dates, so it must not inherit a generic monthly/rolling label.
Never infer rolling intake just because no deadline appears. Preserve email and
download application instructions. Do not create accounts, submit applications or
contact funders.

Check instrument and applicant restrictions: grants are not loans/equity; paid
membership, sanctioned-country restrictions, individual versus organisation rules,
fiscal sponsorship, government leads and restricted research networks matter.
Retain unresolved source/portal conflicts explicitly and withhold actionable claims.
UAF-Africa Rapid Response and Advocacy ceilings currently conflict between its FAQ
and separate forms; do not silently apply those amounts to its Protection route.

Deduplicate official programme/current cycle before writes. Register only the exact
independently checked official source path. Use guarded updates and the existing
`record_funding_status_check` RPC; preserve staff content, verified application links,
deadline history and metadata. Re-read the resulting rows and audits.

Save dated evidence with query groups, candidate exclusions, source/application
retrieval results, published cutoffs, changed fields, audit checks and failures.
No valid new call should be replaced with a weaker discovery just to raise counts.
